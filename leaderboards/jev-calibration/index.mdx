Twenty-three decision models, scored on accuracy, expected calibration error (ECE) and the Brier score. Every model was asked exactly the same questions. Pick a dataset to see every model on it, or a model to see its reliability diagram.

<JevBoard />

## Models

Fifteen models are hosted: TypeSafe's Jev 1.13 and 1.14 and thirteen decision models from Perplexity, Nace AI, Liquid AI, OpenAI, Cloudflare, Inception and Upstage. We asked them through OpenRouter in October 2026, each pinned to its maker's own servers. Each name links to the model's OpenRouter page.

Eight models have open weights. JEV-9B and JEV-27B are AutoTrust's open copies of Jev, trained on Jev 1.13's answers. Qwen3.5-9B, untrained, is the same base model read out the same way without that training. The rest are Jev-style models from other teams, each run through the server its authors ship. Each name links to its model card. Open models were asked with lettered options, the format Jev's documentation uses, so a model tuned to another format may score a little lower here than in its authors' own tests.

## Same questions for everyone

MMLU and MMLU-Pro are cut to 2,000 questions each and BANKING77 to 1,000, sampled in proportion to subject. Every other dataset is used in full. A few models could not answer everything:

- **Solar Decide and Solar Decide Flash** take at most 26 options, so they skipped Under Pressure at 64 options and BANKING77.
- **GPT-6 Luna Decisions** refused 5 questions in the summary datasets.
- **JEV-27B** ran out of GPU memory on MMLU-Pro, BANKING77 and Under Pressure at 64 options.

None of those datasets are in the summary. Jev and GPT-6 Luna round their probabilities to two decimals, which barely moves accuracy, ECE or the Brier score.
