The benchmarks AutoTrust and NeoHorse report for their models, scored for calibration as well as accuracy: Kev's decision-v7, transfer-v4 and transfer-v9 suites, VitaminC and MASSIVE, and the original and easy tiers of JevBench. Every reference answer comes from people or from rules. Two more benchmarks, JevBench's hard tier and Nimble, have answers written by language models; they are shown below but left out of the summary.

Questions come in three kinds. Multiple choice picks one of several options, yes/no asks whether a statement holds, and rating places something on an ordered scale. Pick a kind to see how each model handles it.

## How the models were asked

Each server model was sent every question in its native form. JEV models answered multiple-choice and yes/no questions through their own decision slots. Their rating slots are a fixed 0 to 5 scale without level descriptions, so rating questions were asked as multiple choice over the level descriptions.

Kev-4B was trained on decision-v7's training split, so the decision-v7 partitions are in-distribution for it.
